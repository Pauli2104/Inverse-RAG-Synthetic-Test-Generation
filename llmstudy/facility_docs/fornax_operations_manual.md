# Fornax Building — Operations Manual

Document ID: AUR-FOR-OPS · Revision 4.2 · Effective 2026-03-01 · Owner: Facility Management

## 1. Scope

This manual applies to all rooms and technical installations of the Fornax building on Aurora Campus. Where this manual conflicts with an earlier revision, this revision prevails. Values in the visitor guide are indicative only.

## 2. Rooms

| Room | Max. occupancy (persons) | Default cooling set-point (°C) | Access badge | CO₂ alarm threshold (ppm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hall F24 | 92 | 24.0 | red | 800 |
| Lab F15 | 84 | 23.5 | green | 1400 |
| Lounge F14 | 116 | 21.0 | green | 800 |
| Studio F12 | 28 | 22.5 | red | 1000 |

Occupancy limits are fire-safety limits and must not be exceeded, including during events. Badge levels are cumulative only where stated by Security; a green badge does not grant access to amber, red or black areas.

## 3. Indoor climate

- Hall F24: the room controller targets 24.0 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.
- Lab F15: the room controller targets 23.5 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.
- Studio F12: the room controller targets 22.5 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.

## 4. Technical installations and maintenance

| Installation | Planned maintenance window | Incident escalation |
|---|---|---|
| HVAC | every Saturday from 21:00 to 23:00 | IT operations lead |
| access control | every Thursday from 06:00 to 08:00 | IT operations lead |
| lighting | every Friday from 08:00 to 10:00 | duty engineer |

During a maintenance window the installation may be unavailable. Lifts under maintenance are marked out of service.
