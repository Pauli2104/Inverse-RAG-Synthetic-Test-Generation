# Borealis Building — Operations Manual

Document ID: AUR-BOR-OPS · Revision 4.2 · Effective 2026-03-01 · Owner: Facility Management

## 1. Scope

This manual applies to all rooms and technical installations of the Borealis building on Aurora Campus. Where this manual conflicts with an earlier revision, this revision prevails. Values in the visitor guide are indicative only.

## 2. Rooms

| Room | Max. occupancy (persons) | Default cooling set-point (°C) | Access badge | CO₂ alarm threshold (ppm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hall B44 | 52 | 23.5 | black | 900 |
| Lab B47 | 64 | 21.0 | red | 1100 |
| Lounge B14 | 92 | 24.0 | green | 1400 |
| Studio B17 | 104 | 20.5 | amber | 900 |

Occupancy limits are fire-safety limits and must not be exceeded, including during events. Badge levels are cumulative only where stated by Security; a green badge does not grant access to amber, red or black areas.

## 3. Indoor climate

- Hall B44: the room controller targets 23.5 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.
- Studio B17: the room controller targets 20.5 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.

## 4. Technical installations and maintenance

| Installation | Planned maintenance window | Incident escalation |
|---|---|---|
| elevator | every Thursday from 21:00 to 23:00 | security desk |
| fire alarm | every Saturday from 20:00 to 22:00 | duty engineer |
| lighting | every Tuesday from 07:00 to 09:00 | duty engineer |

During a maintenance window the installation may be unavailable. Lifts under maintenance are marked out of service.
