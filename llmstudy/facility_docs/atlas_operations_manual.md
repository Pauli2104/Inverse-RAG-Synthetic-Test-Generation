# Atlas Building — Operations Manual

Document ID: AUR-ATL-OPS · Revision 4.2 · Effective 2026-03-01 · Owner: Facility Management

## 1. Scope

This manual applies to all rooms and technical installations of the Atlas building on Aurora Campus. Where this manual conflicts with an earlier revision, this revision prevails. Values in the visitor guide are indicative only.

## 2. Rooms

| Room | Max. occupancy (persons) | Default cooling set-point (°C) | Access badge | CO₂ alarm threshold (ppm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hall A18 | 80 | 21.0 | amber | 800 |
| Lab A41 | 76 | 22.0 | green | 800 |
| Lounge A19 | 112 | 22.5 | amber | 1200 |
| Studio A19 | 36 | 23.5 | amber | 1100 |

Occupancy limits are fire-safety limits and must not be exceeded, including during events. Badge levels are cumulative only where stated by Security; a green badge does not grant access to amber, red or black areas.

## 3. Indoor climate

- Lab A41: the room controller targets 22.0 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.
- Lounge A19: the room controller targets 22.5 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.

## 4. Technical installations and maintenance

| Installation | Planned maintenance window | Incident escalation |
|---|---|---|
| HVAC | every Friday from 20:00 to 22:00 | security desk |
| elevator | every Friday from 22:00 to 24:00 | facility manager |
| fire alarm | every Thursday from 22:00 to 24:00 | security desk |

During a maintenance window the installation may be unavailable. Lifts under maintenance are marked out of service.
