# Equinox Building — Operations Manual

Document ID: AUR-EQU-OPS · Revision 4.2 · Effective 2026-03-01 · Owner: Facility Management

## 1. Scope

This manual applies to all rooms and technical installations of the Equinox building on Aurora Campus. Where this manual conflicts with an earlier revision, this revision prevails. Values in the visitor guide are indicative only.

## 2. Rooms

| Room | Max. occupancy (persons) | Default cooling set-point (°C) | Access badge | CO₂ alarm threshold (ppm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hall E13 | 120 | 22.5 | red | 1000 |
| Lab E37 | 36 | 24.0 | red | 900 |
| Lounge E43 | 120 | 21.0 | amber | 900 |
| Studio E26 | 64 | 21.0 | green | 900 |

Occupancy limits are fire-safety limits and must not be exceeded, including during events. Badge levels are cumulative only where stated by Security; a green badge does not grant access to amber, red or black areas.

## 3. Indoor climate

- Hall E13: the room controller targets 22.5 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.
- Lounge E43: the room controller targets 21.0 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.

## 4. Technical installations and maintenance

| Installation | Planned maintenance window | Incident escalation |
|---|---|---|
| access control | every Saturday from 08:00 to 10:00 | security desk |
| elevator | every Thursday from 22:00 to 24:00 | IT operations lead |
| lighting | every Thursday from 07:00 to 09:00 | security desk |

During a maintenance window the installation may be unavailable. Lifts under maintenance are marked out of service.
