# Cygnus Building — Operations Manual

Document ID: AUR-CYG-OPS · Revision 4.2 · Effective 2026-03-01 · Owner: Facility Management

## 1. Scope

This manual applies to all rooms and technical installations of the Cygnus building on Aurora Campus. Where this manual conflicts with an earlier revision, this revision prevails. Values in the visitor guide are indicative only.

## 2. Rooms

| Room | Max. occupancy (persons) | Default cooling set-point (°C) | Access badge | CO₂ alarm threshold (ppm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hall C31 | 100 | 21.5 | red | 1400 |
| Lab C15 | 116 | 20.5 | black | 1400 |
| Lounge C39 | 20 | 23.0 | black | 1100 |
| Studio C13 | 96 | 23.0 | green | 1000 |

Occupancy limits are fire-safety limits and must not be exceeded, including during events. Badge levels are cumulative only where stated by Security; a green badge does not grant access to amber, red or black areas.

## 3. Indoor climate

- Lab C15: the room controller targets 20.5 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.
- Studio C13: the room controller targets 23.0 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.

## 4. Technical installations and maintenance

| Installation | Planned maintenance window | Incident escalation |
|---|---|---|
| HVAC | every Wednesday from 07:00 to 09:00 | duty engineer |
| fire alarm | every Saturday from 07:00 to 09:00 | duty engineer |
| lighting | every Saturday from 07:00 to 09:00 | IT operations lead |

During a maintenance window the installation may be unavailable. Lifts under maintenance are marked out of service.
