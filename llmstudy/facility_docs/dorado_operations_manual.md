# Dorado Building — Operations Manual

Document ID: AUR-DOR-OPS · Revision 4.2 · Effective 2026-03-01 · Owner: Facility Management

## 1. Scope

This manual applies to all rooms and technical installations of the Dorado building on Aurora Campus. Where this manual conflicts with an earlier revision, this revision prevails. Values in the visitor guide are indicative only.

## 2. Rooms

| Room | Max. occupancy (persons) | Default cooling set-point (°C) | Access badge | CO₂ alarm threshold (ppm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hall D12 | 8 | 21.5 | black | 900 |
| Lab D45 | 76 | 21.5 | green | 1400 |
| Lounge D26 | 108 | 23.0 | black | 1100 |
| Studio D18 | 116 | 21.5 | amber | 1000 |

Occupancy limits are fire-safety limits and must not be exceeded, including during events. Badge levels are cumulative only where stated by Security; a green badge does not grant access to amber, red or black areas.

## 3. Indoor climate

- Studio D18: the room controller targets 21.5 °C by default; occupants may adjust by ±1 °C for 2 hours.

## 4. Technical installations and maintenance

| Installation | Planned maintenance window | Incident escalation |
|---|---|---|
| access control | every Friday from 07:00 to 09:00 | IT operations lead |
| elevator | every Saturday from 08:00 to 10:00 | IT operations lead |
| fire alarm | every Wednesday from 08:00 to 10:00 | duty engineer |

During a maintenance window the installation may be unavailable. Lifts under maintenance are marked out of service.
